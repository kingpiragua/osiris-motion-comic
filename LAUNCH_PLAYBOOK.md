## THE DAILY MARKETING BLOCK (30 min, every day, Days 3–30 — non-negotiable)

This is the whole game. Pinterest organic takes 3–6 months to compound — it's the long engine. Short-form video is the only month-one spike channel. Run both from day 3:

- **~15 min — Pinterest (compounding):** 3–5 **fresh pins** (new graphics per URL, not repins — vary crop, text overlay, signal color; the green/red split natively doubles your pin variants). Video pins outperform: Pinterest is video-first in 2026. Keyword the pin titles like Etsy titles.
- **~15 min — short-form video (spiking):** 1 vertical/day from `reel/clips` + process material. Rotating hooks: page→wallpaper transform · the green→red handoff · "my comic's color system has rules" · planner-as-recovered-file flip-through. Post to TikTok, cross-post IG Reels + YT Shorts. Link in bio → Gumroad (10% fee) not Etsy (let Etsy's own search feed Etsy).
- **Weekly (not daily):** engage 10 min in 2 relevant communities (analog horror, digital planning, Boricua art) as a participant, not a spammer.

**Skip rule:** if a day collapses, the 30-min block survives and production slips. Never the reverse.

## VERIFIER GATES (decisions pre-committed — no vibes)

**📊 Day 14** (n is small — directional only):
- Listings getting **views but no favorites/carts** → thumbnail problem: redo first photo before touching anything else.
- Listings getting **near-zero views** → keyword problem: rewrite title front-load + all 13 tags from Etsy autocomplete.
- **One video outperforming 3× baseline** → make 3 more on that hook this week.
- Email signups < 10 → lead magnet is buried: pin it daily, add it to every video caption.

**📊 Day 30** (write v1.2 from this):
| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Shop views | > 500 total | Healthy cold start — keep cadence |
| First sales | ≥ 3 across shop | Product-market signal — double down on whatever sold |
| Conversion | any listing > 1% | That's your hero — feature it everywhere, make companion products |
| Email list | ≥ 50 | Funnel works — plan monthly TRANSMISSION |
| Zero sales but views > 500 | — | Pricing/thumbnail iteration month, not product expansion |
| Views < 200 AND video flat | — | Distribution problem: month 2 = 80% marketing / 20% product |

**Stop condition (pre-committed):** run the full loop for **90 days** (matches verified 3–6-month reality) before any quit decision. Kill criteria at day 90: < 10 total sales AND < 100 email subscribers AND no video > 10k views → shelve the shop, keep the assets (they remain comic marketing), and the loop's goal reverts to Issue #2.
**Upgrade condition:** any product hits 25 sales → it gets its own product line (editions, seasonal variants) in the next cycle.

**Cost guardrail:** ≤ $25 cash this month, ≤ 1 day on analysis. Hours go to production + the daily block.
