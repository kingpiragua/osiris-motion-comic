## THE FINAL FIVE

### 1. THE SPLIT-EYE — hero sticker, ~3″ wide
- **Source:** page 5, panel 3 (glasses macro, "SIGNAL TRACED").
- **Die-cut:** the glasses silhouette — two round lenses + bridge + hair edge cropped to a soft blob. Instantly readable at laptop-lid distance.
- **Holo treatment:** foil ONLY inside the two lenses — left lens green data-glow, right lens red. Face and frames print matte with white-ink underbase (holo shows only where white is knocked out). The rainbow shift makes the two signals literally fight as the sticker tilts. This is the pack's cover star.

### 2. SPLIT-SIGNAL VEJIGANTE — ~3″ tall ⚠ requires one new generation
- **Concept (merge of vejigante + the Split-Eye device):** a Puerto Rican vejigante carnival mask, hard-split down the center seam — left half phosphor-green with amber falloff, right half oxblood red. Horns tipped in holo; eyes holo.
- **Generation prompt (style bible 7a adapted, no character):**
  > Single vejigante carnival mask, front-facing, centered, symmetrical, on plain black background. Left half lit warm phosphor-green fading to amber, right half cold oxblood-red, hard seam down the center. Multiple curved horns, open fanged mouth, Puerto Rican folk-art patterning fused with circuit traces and Taíno petroglyph marks. Matte risograph print texture, visible halftone. Nervous hand-inked linework, variable weight, heavy blacks. High contrast, sticker art, bold silhouette. --no gloss, midjourney sheen, neon cyberpunk, candy-red, clean vector ink, background clutter
- **QA:** must pass the single-panel test AND read as a vejigante to someone Boricua at arm's length. Regenerate until both.

### 3. NETWORK MANDALA — 2.5″ circle
- **Source:** page 6, ceiling — the red surveillance eye inside the circuit mandala.
- **Die-cut:** perfect circle (cheapest die, strongest read).
- **Holo:** full-bleed red holo through the circuit ring; iris gets the heaviest foil so the pupil "follows" as it tilts. Matte black outer ring with tiny reversed type: `SIGNAL LOCKED. SUBJECT IDENTIFIED.`

### 4. OSIRIS SILHOUETTE — ~3″ tall (merge: cover silhouette + CRT frame)
- **Source:** cover — Skye's back-facing silhouette; recolor the golden beam to phosphor green.
- **Die-cut:** rounded-rectangle CRT/Trinitron shape with a fat bezel; inside, her silhouette stands small against the green light column, scanlines across the whole image.
- **Holo:** the light column and scanline gaps only. Bezel matte black with `TRINITRON` micro-type. Reads as "tiny haunted TV" on a water bottle — the utility/meme slot in the pack.

### 5. DISK TAG — ~2.5″ wide (merge: tag + barcode lockup)
- **Source:** the DISK graffiti glyph (pages 2/4/6 — cleanest instance is the page 6 crate; vector-trace it, don't crop it).
- **Die-cut:** contour around the tag letters.
- **Finish:** NO holo — matte white-on-black, deliberately flat, with barcode + `HP-7 // HUMBOLDT-PARK` underline. Every pack needs the humble one that skaters actually slap on poles; this is the brand's street signature and the cheapest to produce.

**Pack rhythm check (verifier):** 2 green-dominant (#1 left lens/#4), 2 red-dominant (#2 right half/#3), 1 neutral (#5) — the pack IS the two-signal system. 3 holo + 1 partial + 1 matte keeps COGS sane. Shapes: blob, mask, circle, rectangle, letters — no two silhouettes rhyme.

## PRODUCTION

- **Extraction:** crop from PNG → 2× upscale → clean edge halo → add 1/8″ bleed + white-ink underbase layer marking holo knockout zones. Vendor needs: art layer + cut line + white-ink layer per sticker.
- **Vendor:** any die-cut shop that runs **holographic vinyl with white-ink underbase** (Sticker Mule, StickerApp, or a local Chicago printer — "printed locally in Chicago" belongs in the listing). Order singles, assemble packs yourself.
- **Unit economics (typical at qty 100/design):** ~$0.45–0.90/sticker → 5-pack COGS ≈ $3–4.50 + $0.60 packaging. **Sell $14** (+$1.50 shipped in a rigid mailer). Margin ~55–65% after Etsy's ~10%.
- **Packaging = the archive frame:** clear poly bag + printed backing card styled as an EVIDENCE label — `RECOVERED MATERIALS // FILE HP-7 // 5 ITEMS // CLEARANCE: DISK`, barcode, REC ●. The backing card is the sixth design and it's the one that gets photographed for reviews.
- **This is physical:** needs an Etsy shipping profile (flat $1.50 US / free over $20), stamps + rigid mailers on hand. Fulfillment ~10 min/order.
