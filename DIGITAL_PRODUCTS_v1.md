## PRODUCT SPECS

### 1. Two-Signal Phone Wallpaper Pack — $6
The fastest product: it's mostly crops of finished pages.

- **Contents:** 12 wallpapers — 4 GREEN (signal/awe), 4 RED (surveillance/dread), 4 NEUTRAL (amber/noir room tone). Sell the *emotional grammar*: "set your phone to whichever signal owns your day."
- **Sources:** crops from `pages/02_room`, `03_static`, `05_reception`, `06_seen` + a few new generations using master prompts 7a/7b with `[CAMERA ANGLE] = macro / detail, no character` (environment-only variants avoid Skye-face QA entirely).
- **Specs:** 1170×2532 (iPhone), 1440×3200 (Android), both included. Keep clock/notification zone quiet — put the dense symbol geometry in the lower two-thirds.
- **Style hooks:** CRT scanlines + halftone stay visible at phone scale — that texture *is* the brand at thumbnail size. Add subtle archive UI chrome (REC ●, timestamp 2:47 AM, file ID) as a signature.
- **Listings:** green edition ($4), red edition ($4), full pack ($6). One free green wallpaper peeled off as the lead magnet.

### 2. SIGNAL Desktop Wallpaper Set — $8
- **Contents:** 6 desktops (3 green / 3 red) at 4K 16:9 + ultrawide 3440×1440. Include one dual-monitor split: **green screen left, red screen right — the Split-Eye as a desk setup.** That's the hero listing image.
- **Sources:** outpaint existing wide panels to 16:9 / ultrawide (the wide/distant camera panels extend naturally); generate 2–3 fresh environment-only scenes (the Paseo Boricua flag arch in rain, per §5 geographic canon, is the strongest desktop subject you have).
- **Rule that makes it sellable:** obey the discipline rule — one signal per wallpaper, green contained in its source, red ambient. Restraint reads as premium; muddy both-signals reads as AI slop.

### 3. OSIRIS.EXE Issue #1 — Digital Comic — $7
The anchor. Everything else funnels to this.

- **Contents:** full-res PDF of the 7 pages (cover → seen), formatted vertical-scroll; the animated reel (`reel/OSIRIS_Issue01_Reel_NOMUSIC_v3_ANIMATED.mp4`) as a bonus; 2 wallpapers from #1 as a teaser cross-sell.
- **Packaging:** lean into archive-file framing — the PDF opens with a "RECOVERED FILE / CLEARANCE LEVEL" title card, barcode, DSB Labs stamp. The frame device *is* the unboxing experience.
- **Why it matters commercially:** it legitimizes the whole shop. A wallpaper store is a wallpaper store; a wallpaper store attached to an original comic is a *franchise*.

### 4. Barrio Futurism Posters — $6 each / $15 bundle
- **The positioning fix (v1.1):** Etsy poster search is dominated by 10,000-print mega-bundles selling for $10–25. **Do not compete on quantity — you lose.** These sell as *5 distinctive art prints from an original comic universe*, listed individually (5 more search entry points) plus a bundle.
  1. **"NUNCA OLVIDES DE DÓNDE VIENES"** — typographic, Aaron Douglas halo geometry radiating from the text
  2. **"LA VERDAD ESTÁ EN LA MEMORIA"** — companion piece, red signal

  5. **Circuit-eye glyph** — franchise mark as sacred-geometry poster, green or red variant

- **Production:** posters 1–3 and 5 are typography/glyph work (Cover/Marketing tier is allowed here — this IS marketing tier); poster 4 comes from master prompt 7a adapted to exterior.
- **Market note:** Spanish-language affirmation prints and Puerto Rican pride prints are proven niches where mega-bundles *can't* follow — a 10,000-print pack has no cultural specificity. This is the moat.

### 5. ARCHIVE FILE Planner Pages — Printable PDF — $12
The archive-file framing (§1) converts 1:1 into planner design language. Niche planners command higher prices with less competition — this is as niche as planners get.

- **Contents (US Letter + A4, ink-friendly line art + one "full signal" cover sheet):**
  - **Daily log** styled as a recovered file: date field = timestamp, priorities = "DIRECTIVES", notes = "FIELD LOG", top-right REC ●, file ID, barcode footer
  - **Weekly spread** with a **signal tracker** — color each day green (signal/flow), red (surveillance/dread), or amber (neutral): the issue's color rhythm as a mood tracker
  - **Habit tracker** as a "SYSTEM DIAGNOSTICS" grid with CRT scanline row shading
  - **Project page** as "CASE FILE" with clearance-level header
  - **Dot-grid + lined pages** with faint halftone texture and coquí / ankh corner glyphs
- **Production:** pure layout work (Figma/Illustrator/Canva) using the palette + glyphs — no generation needed, so zero face-drift QA. Keep interior pages mostly monochrome so they're printer-friendly; sell the drama on the cover.

## THE TRAFFIC ENGINE (v1.1 — this is what makes month one work)

The verified reality: new Etsy shops take **3–6 months** to reach consistent sales on organic search alone, and external traffic is the single biggest accelerator. Listing is not launching. The month plan therefore ships marketing *with* the products:

1. **Pinterest — daily, non-negotiable.** The best free traffic source for digital-product Etsy shops. Every product yields 3–5 pins (mockup, detail crop, in-situ). The two-signal aesthetic is *made* for Pinterest's dark-academia / analog-horror / Y2K boards. Target: 5 pins/day from day 1 — by day 30 that's 150 pins compounding.
2. **The reel is the ad budget.** `reel/clips` already exists — cut to 15–30s verticals for TikTok / IG Reels / YT Shorts: "turning my comic's style system into products," page-to-wallpaper transforms, the green→red handoff as a visual hook. Process content from a real original comic outperforms product shots.
3. **Free downloads capture emails.** The two lead magnets (free wallpaper, free texture sampler) run on Gumroad $0+ with email required. The list gets one "TRANSMISSION" email per launch week.
4. **Route owned traffic to Gumroad direct links** (10% + $0.50 fee), let cold discovery happen on Etsy (worth its fees) — never link your own audience to listings where the platform takes 30%.
5. **Cross-sell inside every file.** Last page of every PDF = archive-file card linking the comic + the bundle.

**Month-one revenue expectation (honest):** beginners average $200–1,000/month in year one, and month one skews low. The month-one win condition is *infrastructure*: 20+ listings live, both lead magnets running, 100+ pins, first reviews in. Revenue compounds from there.

## PRODUCTION RULES (so the products stay on-model)

1. **The style bible governs products too.** Every asset passes the single-panel test: no title, still unmistakably OSIRIS.EXE. If it violates a locked rule, it's off-model — regenerate, don't ship.
2. **One signal per product variant.** Green edition / red edition is the product line's grammar — it doubles SKUs for free, enforces the discipline rule, and is how 10 products become 23 listings.
3. **Prefer environment-only art for products.** Skye stays in the comic. Products built on the room, the street, the glyphs, and the UI need no character-consistency QA and don't dilute her as a story asset.
4. **Archive-file framing is the packaging.** Every PDF cover, install guide, and listing image is a "recovered file." One packaging concept, ten products.
5. **Never drift into** the §1 negative list — especially "AI pretty art" sheen. Matte, printed, imperfect is the moat; it's what makes these look nothing like the default AI-generated product flood.
6. **Listing images:** dark mockups, CRT glow, phone/desk/wall in situ. Reuse the reel clips as Etsy listing videos — animated listings convert measurably better and you already have the footage.

## THE LOOP PROTOCOL (how this strategy keeps refining itself)

Run the same loop monthly that produced v1.1. The strategy is **State**; this doc's refinement log is **Memory**.

- **Goal:** a product line that sells, on-model, without new de-risking work.
- **Work:** ship the current calendar week.
- **Check (verifier — real data only, no vibes):** at each month's end pull: views per listing, favorites, conversion per listing, which pins drive clicks, email signups per lead magnet, revenue per product.
- **Repeat:** double down on the top-2 sellers (new editions, companion products), fix or kill the bottom-2 (better thumbnails/keywords first — thumbnails before product changes), log the change here as v1.2, v1.3, …
- **Stop condition:** a product line iteration is *done* when top sellers are identified and repeatably restockable — then the loop's goal upgrades (Issue #2 tie-ins, seasonal editions, licensing the texture system).
- **Cost guardrail:** never spend more than 1 day/month on refinement analysis; the loop exists to direct production hours, not consume them.
